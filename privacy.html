Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026 · Last updated August 29, 2026

Vestibrum, Inc. ("Vestibrum," "we," "us") operates vestibrum.com. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than boilerplate.

The short version: vestibrum.com is a brochure site. We set no cookies of our own, run no advertising or analytics trackers, and do not sell or share your personal information. The only personal information we hold is what you deliberately send us.

1. Who we are

Vestibrum, Inc. is a Delaware corporation developing Otoli, a smart infant sleep system. Vestibrum is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at info@vestibrum.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

If you email us, book a meeting with us, or otherwise get in touch, we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, email address, and the contents of your message. If you are contacting us in a professional capacity, that may include your company and role.

Mailing list

The site offers an email signup form for Otoli updates. Submitting it opens a message in your own email application, addressed to us and containing the address you entered — nothing is sent until you send that message yourself. We then keep your address to send occasional updates about Otoli, and for nothing else.

We do not yet use a third-party mailing list provider. If we adopt one, we will update this policy to name it. You can ask us to remove your address at any time by replying to any update or emailing info@vestibrum.com, and we will delete it.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider generates standard server logs when your browser requests a page. These typically include your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, your browser's user-agent string, and the referring page. These logs exist for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. We do not use them to build profiles of visitors and do not attempt to identify individuals from them.

Children

Otoli is a product for infants, but this website is intended for adults — parents, clinicians, investors, and partners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

3. Cookies and tracking

Vestibrum does not set cookies on vestibrum.com. We do not use advertising networks, retargeting pixels, session recording, or third-party analytics.

Because we set no cookies and run no trackers, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals — there is nothing for them to turn off.

4. Third parties that may receive data

A small number of third parties are involved in delivering the site. Each has its own privacy policy governing what it does with data it receives.

  • Railway — our hosting provider. Processes server requests and logs as described above.
  • Google Fonts — the site loads typefaces from Google's font CDN. As a result, your browser makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address and user-agent. We receive nothing from Google in return, and no Google cookie is set by this request.
  • Calendly — if you follow a scheduling link from our site and book a meeting, Calendly collects the information you enter into its booking form and shares it with us. That interaction is governed by Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Email providers — messages you send to a vestibrum.com address are processed and stored by our email provider.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. How we use information

We use the information described above to reply to your message, to evaluate partnership or investment interest, to maintain and secure the site, and to comply with legal obligations. That is the extent of it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed for the purpose it was sent, and thereafter as required for legal, tax, or recordkeeping obligations. Server logs are retained on our hosting provider's standard schedule, which is short — typically measured in days to weeks. You can ask us to delete correspondence at any time.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We limit access to correspondence to people at Vestibrum who need it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — please do not send us sensitive personal or health information by email.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with comprehensive privacy laws, as well as individuals in the UK and EEA, have these or similar rights.

Because we hold very little — generally only correspondence you initiated — most requests are simple to fulfill. Email info@vestibrum.com and we will respond within the timeframe the applicable law requires. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

9. International visitors

Vestibrum is based in the United States and our service providers are principally located there. If you contact us from outside the United States, the information you send will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as the site and the business change — in particular, when the mailing list becomes active or when we add analytics. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be described at the top of the page.
